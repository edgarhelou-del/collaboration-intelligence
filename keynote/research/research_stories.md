## PART A — CORPORATE ADAPTABILITY STORIES
1. 1997 Aisin Fire — Toyota supply chain rebuilt in ~5 days via distributed self-organization of 200+ firms, no central plan. Sources: Nishiguchi & Beaudet, Sloan Mgmt Review Fall 1998 p.49 (https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=1762242); thecasecentre.org; en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1997_Aisin_fire
2. Toyota Andon Cord — any line worker can trigger a stop; distributed sensing built into the system (Jidoka). Sources: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andon_(manufacturing); leanblog.org/2012/11/andon-cords-toyota-takaota-speaking-up; mag.toyota.co.uk/andon-toyota-production-system
3. Nvidia's pivot gaming→AI computing — 6-year unmonetized CUDA bet (2006) validated by AlexNet 2012, a decade pre-ChatGPT. Sources: computerhistory.org/profile/jensen-huang; sequoiacap.com/podcast/crucible-moments-nvidia; entrepreneur.com/.../477530; cbsnews.com 60 Minutes
4. Netflix DVD→streaming — launched streaming 2007 while DVD still profitable; deliberately excluded DVD-division staff from strategy meetings to force organizational attention shift (Sarandos). DVD service ended Sept 29 2023. Sources: hollywoodreporter.com/.../1235402250; colemaninsights.com blog; deadline.com gallery
5. Spotify Squad Model — 2012 Kniberg & Ivarsson whitepaper (snapshot, not prescription), copied worldwide; 2020 Jeremiah Lee exposé shows Spotify itself quietly abandoned it — autonomy without coordination caused duplicated stacks & power struggles. GOOD COUNTER-EXAMPLE (decentralization ≠ automatically good). Sources: jeremiahlee.com/posts/failed-squad-goals; realkm.com/2020/12/06/...; businessofsoftware.org/2020/06/...

## PART C — AI CHANGING EXECUTION (2023-2025)
1. Base44 — solo founder Maor Shlomo built AI app-builder, $1M ARR in 3 weeks, sold to Wix for $80M cash (+$90M milestones) after 6 months, no VC funding, essentially no team. Sources: calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/s1iflnlelx; dealroom.co news feed; lennysnewsletter.com Base44 story
2. GitHub Copilot RCT — Peng, Kalliamvakou, Cihon, Demirer (2023) — randomized trial: developers with Copilot completed task 55.8% faster, biggest gains for less-experienced devs. arxiv.org/abs/2302.06590
3. Klarna AI assistant — Feb 2024: handled 2.3M chats in month 1 (=700 FTE workload), cut resolution time 11min→2min; 2025: CEO Siemiatkowski admitted "cut too far too fast," quality suffered, rehired humans. GOOD NUANCE STORY. Sources: forbes.com/sites/jackkelly/2024/03/04/...; fastcompany.com/91468582/...
